Org health & manager spans

Finance and HR count "management overhead" differently. org_health_360 fixes the definitions of span of control and overspan once, from the reporting lines already in the data.

Widest spans of control

Employee voice: the unstructured layer

Stay and exit interview transcripts are scored inside Snowflake by a Snowpark Python procedure (SCORE_VOICE_TRANSCRIPTS) using Cortex sentiment and multi-label reason classification against a fixed taxonomy — not keyword matching.

Reasons given — primary label per interview

Where voice and metrics agree

Negative interviews from people the structured metric already flags as flight risks (underpaid, rated 4+). Expand for the transcript and the review behind it.

Disclosure: 5 of the 18 synthetic interviews were deliberately placed on employees who already meet the flight-risk rule, so the cross-signal is demonstrable. The sentiment scores and reason labels are Cortex's own output.

Comp band architecture

Bands get set once and drift. band_health_360 audits whether people are actually paid inside their own published ranges — and whether the ranges separate the levels at all.

By band

Range overlap between adjacent bands

0% means the ranges are disjoint; 100% means identical. At 71%, pay alone can't tell an M1 manager from an IC5.

Guardrail: the auditor and its audit_comp_bands MCP tool report findings only. There is no proposed-pay column and no way to ask for one — a human sets the band.

Governance that can't quietly drift

Every governed metric has an owner, a certified definition and a golden-value test. The suite runs on a schedule, so a changed definition — or a changed Cortex model — fails loudly instead of silently moving a number in a board pack.

Scheduled tasks

Latest governance alerts

The suite passes with HIGH alerts open: those aren't test failures, they're the schedulers correctly escalating a real pay-equity breach and people paid below their band minimum.

What's actually running underneath

Five governed Semantic Views

employee_360, org_health_360, pay_equity_360, employee_voice_360, band_health_360. Every governed question resolves through a view, never a raw table, and each answers in Cortex Analyst.

Snowpark Python + Cortex AI

SCORE_VOICE_TRANSCRIPTS runs inside Snowflake, calling Cortex sentiment and AI_CLASSIFY on 18 transcripts and writing governed results.

Two reusable CoCo CLI skills

$workforce-astra-data-gen generates, verifies and loads the data; $workforce-astra-governance-check runs the regression suite and reports tasks and alerts.

Mock MCP action server

Six tools — manager alert, comp-adjustment draft, promotion nomination, requisition, guarded reorg simulator, and a report-only band auditor. dry_run defaults to true; nothing is sent.

Cortex Search

Live service, indexing and serving both active over 127 review notes (arctic-embed-m-v1.5), returning the cited review text behind a flight-risk call.

Scheduled governance

Three Snowflake Tasks: a daily 25-test regression run, a weekly pay-equity drift check, and a quarterly band review. Streamlit-in-Snowflake portal on top.
